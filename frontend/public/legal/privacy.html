1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information you give us

In Short: You do not have to give us anything to use the Services.

There is no account, no sign-up, no password and no payment. The only way you deliberately send us personal information is by writing to us: if you email {{contactEmail}}, we receive your address and whatever you put in the message.

Information the Services receive automatically

In Short: Some information reaches us because your browser or your device asked our server for something.

  • The zone or place you choose. Selecting a bidding zone on the map sends us that zone. In the Shelly setup wizard you can ask your browser to locate you instead; if you grant that permission, your approximate coordinates are sent to our zone-resolution endpoint, used to work out which bidding zone the point falls in, and discarded when the response is returned. They are never written to our database.
  • What a connected device asks for. A Shelly script or Home Assistant integration requests a schedule with a device identifier that is generated on your side, a bidding zone code, how many hours the job needs and when it must be finished. The identifier is an opaque string we never link to a person, and the request is not stored.
  • Technical log data. Your IP address, the URL requested, the time, the response status and your browser’s user-agent string, recorded by our web server and by Azure Application Insights. Your IP address is also the key our rate limiter counts requests against, so that one caller cannot exhaust the API for everyone else.

What we do not collect

  • No special category data. We do not process any of the categories protected by Article 9 GDPR. Electricity consumption timing is not one of them, and we do not receive meter readings in any case.
  • No account or contract data. {{siteName}} is not your energy supplier, holds no supply contract for you, and has no login to attach anything to.
  • Nothing in our database points back at you. The only records we keep are public wholesale electricity prices per bidding zone and outdoor temperature readings per zone. Neither is personal data.

6. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

In Short: Technical logs for 90 days. Email for as long as the conversation is useful. Nothing else.

  • Web server access logs and Azure Application Insights telemetry, including IP addresses: 90 days, after which they are deleted automatically by the platform.
  • Rate limiter counters keyed on IP address: held in memory for the length of the one-minute window and never written to disk.
  • Coordinates sent to resolve a bidding zone: used for the duration of the request and then discarded. They are never stored.
  • Schedule requests from your devices: answered and discarded. The schedule is computed and returned; nothing about the request is kept beyond the access log entry above.
  • Email you send us: kept while the matter is live and for up to 24 months afterwards, so that we can pick a thread back up.
  • Spot prices and temperature readings: kept indefinitely, because they are public market and weather data and are not personal data.

When we no longer have a reason to hold personal information, we delete it or anonymise it. Where that is not immediately possible, because it sits in a backup for instance, we isolate it from further processing until deletion is possible.
